Research Interests

Where geography, data, and cities meet

Five threads run through my work — from raw satellite pixels to the lived experience of urban heat.

Geospatial Analysis

Exploring spatial patterns in environmental and urban data using GIS techniques and spatial statistics.

Remote Sensing

Utilizing satellite imagery to monitor land cover changes, urban expansion, and environmental indicators.

Climate Change

Analyzing climate trends and urban heat islands to support sustainability and adaptation strategies.

Environmental Monitoring

Mapping and assessing ecological changes and ecosystem health using multi-source geospatial data.

Urban Sustainability

Designing resilient urban spaces through integrated planning, green infrastructure, and equity mapping.

See this research in practice

The NYC Tree Census Map applies several of these threads — remote sensing, environmental data, and urban planning — in one interactive tool.
