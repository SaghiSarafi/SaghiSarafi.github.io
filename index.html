About

Mapping cities, climate, and change

I'm a spatial data analyst and urban designer with a background spanning urban planning, urban design, and spatial information management across Iran, Austria, and the United States. My current research focuses on urban heat islands and remote sensing at the University of New Mexico, building on earlier work digitizing building footprints in Austria and leading spatial analysis and urban development projects in Tehran, Iran.

Geospatial Analysis

Spatial patterns in environmental and urban data using GIS techniques and spatial statistics.

Remote Sensing

Satellite imagery for monitoring land cover change, urban expansion, and environmental indicators.

Urban Sustainability

Resilient urban spaces through integrated planning, green infrastructure, and equity mapping.
